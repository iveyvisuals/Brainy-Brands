Full PPC Build-Out & Management

This is the service the 25% ad spend reduction guarantee attaches to. Campaigns built and managed by hand, with Amazon's dynamic bidding and placement multipliers turned off.

What gets built

Most accounts we take over are running a campaign structure built for a much smaller catalog, or one an agency set up once during launch and never revisited. We rebuild it around how the account actually looks today — separating campaigns by match type and by ASIN so each bid can be moved independently instead of dragging a group of unrelated keywords with it.

Search terms get reviewed on a real cadence, not once at setup. Wasted spend gets cut into negative keywords before it compounds. Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, and Sponsored Display are coordinated so they're not quietly bidding against each other for the same shopper.

What gets managed, every week

The build-out is the easy part. The management is the actual work: dynamic bidding down to fixed, placement multipliers to zero, and every bid walked up or down by hand based on what it's actually doing to volume and cost — not on what an algorithm optimizing for Amazon's own numbers decides to do with it.

This runs every week for as long as the account is ours, which is the reason we cap how many accounts we take on.

What this service is backed by

25% ad spend reduction in 30 days, scaling toward 50% by day 60–90 — or you don't pay.

Available to accounts spending $10K+/month on Amazon ads, after a qualifying audit. If we miss it, you get your money back or we keep working free until we hit it.
