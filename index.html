What we do differently

Amazon's default settings let Amazon decide what you pay. Dynamic bidding adjusts your bid in real time. Placement multipliers add a percentage on top. Both are on by default, and both work in Amazon's favor, not yours.

We turn them off. Then we set every bid manually, top down, and manage them by hand from there. It's slower work than letting an algorithm run it — that's the point. It's also why accounts we take over usually spend less within the first month.
