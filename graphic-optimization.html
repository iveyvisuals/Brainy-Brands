Graphic Optimization

A close look at every image on your SKUs to find what's costing you conversions, then fixing it.

What we're actually looking at

Most listings lose sales in the image set before a shopper ever reads a word of copy. We check the main image against what's actually required and against what the category leaders are doing, then work through the secondary images in order — whether the sequence answers the questions a shopper has, in the order they'd ask them.

A large share of Amazon traffic is on a phone, so we test legibility at thumbnail size specifically, not just at full resolution. Text that reads fine on a desktop monitor is often unreadable in a search grid.

Where this fits with the rest of the listing

Graphic optimization is priced separately from full account management — see the Creative Optimization tier on the services page for starting rates. It's often done alongside A+ content and 3D rendering, since all three come out of the same audit of what's actually holding a listing back.

That's what the audit is for. Twenty minutes with Dale in your live account and you'll know what's worth fixing first.
