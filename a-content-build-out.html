A+ Content Build-Out

Finding where A+ content can do more, then building it. Done well it moves both conversion rate and sales.

What we build

We look at what's actually missing from the current modules — comparison charts against your own catalog, the brand story a shopper needs before they'll trust a listing they've never seen, and the spec or feature call-outs that a photo alone can't communicate. Then we build the modules that are actually missing, not a generic template swapped in with new photos.

Every build gets checked on a phone screen before it ships. A module that only renders correctly on desktop isn't finished.

Where it fits with the rest of the listing

A+ content is priced separately from full account management — see the Creative Optimization tier on the services page for starting rates. Examples of finished A+ build-outs are just below.
