Inventory Management

Inventory planning and shipment build-outs, whether goods ship from a 3PL or direct from a factory in China. We'll coordinate the logistics.

What gets coordinated

Reorder timing set against actual sell-through rate, not a fixed calendar reminder. FBA shipment plans built and split correctly across fulfillment centers. And a real eye on avoiding both ends of the problem — long-term storage fees on inventory that's sitting too long, and stockouts on inventory that isn't ordered soon enough.

Whether your goods move through a 3PL or ship direct from a factory, we work against the lead times you actually have, not a generic shipping estimate.

Why this has to sit next to PPC, not apart from it

A stockout in the middle of a scaling ad campaign doesn't just pause sales — it wastes the ad spend that was driving traffic to a listing that can't fulfill, and it can knock down organic rank that takes weeks to earn back. Inventory planning has to happen alongside the ad push, not as a separate conversation that only comes up after something runs out.
