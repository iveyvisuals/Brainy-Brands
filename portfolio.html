Amazon advanced certifications

Current, and held directly — not by a subcontractor.

Amazon Ads Campaign Optimization

Structuring and tuning Sponsored Products, Brands, and Display campaigns — bid strategy, placement multipliers, and keyword harvesting that actually move ACoS instead of just click volume.

Amazon Advertising Foundations

The baseline certification covering how Amazon's ad auction works, how the ad types differ, and how targeting and budgets interact. Everything else builds on this.

Amazon DSP Advanced

Programmatic display and video, on and off Amazon — remarketing to shoppers who viewed a listing but didn't buy, and reaching audiences before they ever search.

Amazon Marketing Cloud

Amazon's clean-room analytics tool. Shows how Sponsored Ads, DSP, and organic traffic actually work together across a customer's path to purchase, instead of guessing from five separate reports.

Sponsored Brands video and OTT placements on Fire TV — video ad formats that show up in search results and on streaming devices, not just banner space.

Amazon Ads Advanced Retail

Advanced strategy for retail-specific catalogs — multi-SKU brands, seasonal assortments, and the bid logic that's different when you're managing dozens of ASINs instead of one hero product.

Case studies

Five accounts, with the before-and-after numbers as reported by Amazon Business Reports.

ClearShield

+119% revenue in the first month

$41,816 → $91,572/month, on nearly flat ad spend.

TACoS fell from 5.50% to 2.58% while the flagship ASIN's conversion rate jumped 54%. The growth came from monetizing existing traffic better, not from spending more to get it.

Patriot Crew

$174K → $388K/month

+135% average monthly sales in the first month of management.

An already-healthy account that had plateaued — the ceiling was a campaign structure built for a much smaller catalog. Growth showed up immediately and held through month two.

HIKERS Co.

+104% conversion, in 30 days

4.02% → 8.20% on a core ASIN.

Sessions fell 51% as traffic normalized, yet sales held almost flat — down just 1.2%. The listing kept nearly all of its revenue on half the traffic, a stronger base to scale from.

Primal Power Liver

+33% sales, month over month

$7,993 → $10,642/month, the first full month after onboarding.

The account had softened during the July transition. By August it had recovered and then some — sales, sessions, and units all finished above the pre-onboarding baseline.

Emerald Plastics

$2,381 → $6,546/month

+175% sales from a cold launch, in two months.

No sales history, no reviews, no ranking to start. Orders more than doubled and units grew 164% as traffic scaled toward the best-performing ASINs.
