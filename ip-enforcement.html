IP Enforcement

If you hold IP on your products, we monitor for infringement and file claims on your behalf through Amazon.

What we watch for

Listing hijackers riding your existing detail page, counterfeit sellers undercutting on a product that isn't actually yours to sell, and competitors lifting registered trademark language or brand assets into their own listings. Each of these costs you sales directly, and a hijacked listing can also cost you the buy box while it's happening.

How claims actually get filed

Through Amazon Brand Registry, backed by test-buy evidence where it's needed rather than a report with no proof behind it. This service only applies if your brand already holds a registered trademark or other protectable IP — if you're not sure whether you qualify, that's a five-minute conversation, not a blocker.
